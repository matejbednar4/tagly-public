## Customer & Instagram bans

Customers have a `status` (`ACTIVE | BANNED`, default `ACTIVE`). A BANNED customer can still log in but cannot perform value-bearing actions — enforce this with `assertCustomerNotBannedOrThrow(customerId)` (`src/utils/customers/`) at the top of any such controller (currently create-share and create-withdrawal; add it to new ones).

`BannedInstagram` is a persistent block-list keyed on `instagramUid`, so a banned person can't re-use the same Instagram (delete account + re-register, or move the IG to another account). Check it with `assertInstagramNotBannedOrThrow(instagramUid, tx?)` **wherever an IG is assigned/reassigned to a Tagly account**. It lives in `src/utils/instagram/` and is wired into:

- **`createCustomerWithUniqueUid`** — the single chokepoint for all registration paths. The util receives `instagramDbId` (not the uid), so it resolves the `instagramUid` from the linked `CustomerInstagramAccount` row inside the transaction, then asserts; a banned IG rolls back the whole creation.
- every connect path (link, reconnect, re-auth) in **`connectInstagram`** (`POST /v2/customers/me/instagram/connect`).

Do **not** add it to plain login of an already-bound account.

The admin endpoint `PATCH /v2/admin/customers/:uid/banned` (`setCustomerBanned`) is the single ban/unban action: it flips `status` and adds/removes the `BannedInstagram` row, and always emails the customer (`c.account.banned` / `c.account.unbanned`, mandatory email-only).

**Ban scope + the `customerUid` pointer (E059, decided 2026-08-02).** The ban blocklists the IG connected **at that moment** — this is INTENDED: there is no IG link-history table (`instagram_uid` exists only on `customer_instagram_accounts` and `banned_instagrams` itself), so an IG disconnected before the ban is not blocklisted, by design. Don't "fix" this without a schema decision from Matej. What must NOT regress: every `BannedInstagram` row carries a `customerUid` audit pointer, and **unban + both ban-reason lookups (`hydrateCustomerMe`, `adminHydrateCustomer`) match by that pointer OR the currently-linked uid — never by the deletable `customer.instagram` relation alone.** Relation-only keying orphaned blocklist rows forever on unban (the endpoint is the table's only writer) and rendered reason-less ban banners after a disconnect/rotation.

**The two ban error codes are named in `ERROR_CODES.DOMAIN`** (`@tagly/models/shared`): `CUSTOMER_BANNED` (1540, thrown by `assertCustomerNotBannedOrThrow`) and `INSTAGRAM_BANNED` (1541, `assertInstagramNotBannedOrThrow`). Throw via the constant, not the literal. The client maps both **centrally** in `tagly-shared/client/api/caller.ts` (`showFriendlyError`) to a localized alert, so every banned-gated call site (connect/reconnect IG, create-share, withdrawal) surfaces the right message with no per-screen wiring.

**Client-facing ban state:** `GET /v2/customers/me` (`hydrateCustomerMe`) exposes `status` and `banReason` on `CustomerMe`. `banReason` is looked up from the `BannedInstagram` row (by the linked `instagramUid`) **only when `status === "BANNED"`**, and degrades to `null` on any miss (session hydrators never throw). The customer app renders a banned banner (account hub) + a drawer pill from these. A general-purpose **contact-support** flow posts to `POST /v2/cross/support` (`submitSupport`, twin of `submitFeedback` → emails `CONTACT.SUPPORT_EMAIL` with an optional origin `context` tag).

## Share approval gate

Every share carries an `approvalStatus` (`PENDING | APPROVED | REJECTED`, default `PENDING`; legacy rows backfilled to `APPROVED`), orthogonal to the pipeline `status`. New shares are created `PENDING` and are **fully hidden from the advertiser** — no budget lock, no `a.shares.created` notification, and excluded from every advertiser-facing query/stat/aggregation. The canonical form of that filter — and of **every other share-axis predicate** — is **`utils/shares/shareAccess.ts`** (the share twin of `businessAccessibility.ts`): spread `ADMIN_APPROVED_SHARE_WHERE` into a Prisma `where`, or `filterAdminApprovedShares(rows)` / `isAdminApprovedShare(row)` over already-fetched rows. Use it instead of re-inlining `approvalStatus: "APPROVED"`. Customer reads are unfiltered (ownership is the filter — their PENDING/REJECTED shares render via the derived status). Internal cap/limit/dedupe queries (`LIVE_SHARE_WHERE`) deliberately still count `PENDING` shares (conservative), and **platform-wide** statistics use the looser `NOT_REJECTED_SHARE_WHERE` (PENDING still counts as created; a VALID report counts as rejected, mirroring the derived customer status) — that is deliberately NOT the advertiser-visible gate; both live in `shareAccess.ts` so picking the wrong one is a visible choice, not a typo.

Admins moderate via `GET /v2/admin/shares/pending-approval` (v2 admin, `controllers/v2/adminApp/shares/`), then:

- **`POST /v2/admin/shares/:uid/approve`** (`approveShare`) — atomically flips to `APPROVED` and places the deferred lock (CREDITS/SUBSIDIZED via `lockBudgetForShare`; PAYG lock-less), then sends `a.shares.created`. CREDITS locks are **capped at the then-available budget** (`capAtAvailableBudget`) — a thin budget yields a partial lock; settlement still pays the customer **in full**, drawing the advertiser's available budget negative when the lock doesn't cover the final price (the receivable — E069 policy, 2026-08-01). Locks have a **1-cent minimum** so zero-estimate shares stay approvable (settlement requires the SHARE_LOCK event as its currency source of truth). Only if the lock can't be placed at all (budget fully drained → 1516, platform balance too low → 1517) does the tx roll back and the share stay `PENDING`.
- **`POST /v2/admin/shares/:uid/reject`** (`rejectShare`) — sets `approvalStatus = REJECTED` only (the payout `status` stays `PROCESSING` — rejection is NOT a status in v2; the payment worker requires `approvalStatus = APPROVED`, so a rejected share is never paid), penalizes the customer's multiplier (`SHARE_REJECTION_PENALTY_BPS`), keeps the row + media, and sends the mandatory push+email `c.shares.rejected`. **Rejection is the only way to kill a bad share — there is no hard-delete endpoint.**

**Both approve and reject are also guarded on `status = "PROCESSING"`** (the pre-check *and* the atomic `updateMany` claim), and the pending-approval queue filters to it. A share can be voided to `UNPAYABLE` while still `approvalStatus = PENDING` — the insights/snapshot workers' max-attempts backstop, or a customer Instagram disconnect (`markShareUnpayableAndUnlock`). Approving such a dead share would place a real budget lock the payment worker (which requires `status = PROCESSING`) can never settle or release → trapped funds; rejecting it would clobber `UNPAYABLE → REJECTED` after the money logic ran and re-penalize the customer. The guard makes both a 409 and drops voided shares out of the moderation queue.

The payment worker (`findSharesReadyForPayment`) requires `approvalStatus = "APPROVED"` and `createdAt ≤ now − 24h` (`minPaymentAgeMs`), guaranteeing the review window without touching the postedToIgAt-based insights/snapshot scheduling.

## The v2 admin moderation surface (Review tab)

`GET /v2/admin/review/counts` (queue sizes, filters identical to the lists), `GET /v2/admin/shares/{pending-report,unrated}`, `PUT /v2/admin/shares/:uid/report-review` (verdict CAS on the report row; VALID releases the held lock via the shared correct-pool `releaseShareLockIfAny` — the same helper `markShareUnpayableAndUnlock` uses — and applies the escalating `REPORT_PENALTIES`; DISMISSED requires an admin star rating), `POST /v2/admin/shares/:uid/assess` (admin twin of the advertiser assessment, SUBSIDIZED-only), and `GET /v2/admin/links/pending` + `POST /v2/admin/links/:id/review` (the only writer of `Link.status` — approval puts a business link live for customers; rejection is the global kill-switch).

**Every share-axis predicate lives in `utils/shares/shareAccess.ts`** (the share twin of `businessAccessibility.ts`) — one named rule per shape (Prisma where · Kysely/SQL fragment · in-memory twin): `ADMIN_APPROVED_SHARE_*` (advertiser-visible), `UNASSESSED_SHARE_WHERE` (`ratedAt IS NULL` ⟺ unrated AND unreported), `NOT_REJECTED_SHARE_WHERE`/`shareNotRejectedSql`/`isRejectedLikeShare` (platform "created" gate — the two-part rejected-like rule = intake-REJECTED OR VALID report, in Prisma/SQL/in-memory shapes; **the `derive*ShareStatus` functions compose `isRejectedLikeShare`** so the customer/advertiser-facing status can never disagree with the query gate), `LIVE_SHARE_WHERE`/`shareLiveSql` + `shareDeadSql` (live vs voided/rejected-like — caps, dedupe, stuck-budget recovery, purge guards), `PENDING_ADMIN_APPROVAL_WHERE` (+`_QUEUE_`) (the approval queue AND the approve/reject CAS guard, so they can't disagree), `ADMIN_ASSESSABLE_SHARE_WHERE`, `PENDING_REPORT_WHERE`/`SHARE_HAS_PENDING_REPORT_WHERE`, `NO_PENDING_OR_VALID_REPORT_WHERE` + `isPendingOrValidReport` (the payment worker's scan gate AND the in-claim atomic re-check), `PAID_SHARE_WHERE`/`sharePaidSql` (money-final earnings stats), and `PROCESSING_SHARE_WHERE`/`isProcessingShare` (in-flight — the void/purge filters + the settle/void CAS guards). Never re-inline a share-axis literal in a query — compose these.

**The only sanctioned raw literals left are state-transition *write* values** (`data: { status: "PAID" }`, `approvalStatus: "APPROVED"`, the creation stamp) — you assign a state, you don't filter it. **Deliberately moderation-agnostic reads (documented in-file, do NOT add a gate):** the reward base-price reach history + the frequency dampener (`utils/pricing/helpers/*` — IG-reported reach/post-count is a fact regardless of quality; filtering would let rejected shares be farmed), the visited-businesses aggregate (earnings come from the ledger, a rejected share is $0 there), and `getUnsharedLiveStories` (dedups against the `monetized_instagram_posts` registry — see below). The once-only assessment write itself is centralized in **`utils/shares/claimShareAssessment.ts`** (shared by the advertiser `submitShareAssessment` and admin `assessShare`). Every multiplier-moving moderation path bumps `bumpCustomerRewardEstGen` after commit.

## Once-only story monetization — the `monetized_instagram_posts` registry (E074)

A story is monetizable ONCE, EVER — even if its share was voided or rejected. The single dedup source is the append-only `MonetizedInstagramPost` table: share creation (`createShare`) inserts the `igPostId` in the same transaction (PK conflict → 1013), and `getUnsharedLiveStories` filters the picker against it. Rows are **never deleted**, and the table deliberately has **no customer link**: GDPR/Meta erasure (`deleteCustomerInstagramData`) nulls `Share.igPostId` on every share *including PAID* (de-links the post from the person) while the registry keeps the once-only rule alive — and PAID rows **retain `postedToIgAt`** there so the frequency dampener survives a self-service data-deletion round-trip (the E074 reward-inflation exploit). Never re-derive "already monetized" from `Share.igPostId` (it's an operational column for the insights worker/admin display), and never add a delete path to the registry.

Related: **voiding a share always goes through `markShareUnpayableAndUnlock`** (the single UNPAYABLE writer), whose `reason` is a typed **`ShareVoidReason` slug** (`@tagly/models`) stamped to `Share.voidReason` and rendered as localized copy on the v2 customer/advertiser share details — put raw error text in `detail` (→ `lastProcessingError`, admin-only), never in `reason`.
