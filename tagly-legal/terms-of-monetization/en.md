## 2. Eligibility

You may create a Share only if, at the time of creation:

- your Tagly account is active and in good standing (not suspended or banned),
- your Instagram account is connected with valid permissions,
- the content is **Eligible Content**: a format offered for monetization in the app — currently an Instagram **Story** — that is **currently live** on your connected Instagram account and was posted by you personally (not uploaded, reposted, or managed by a third party on your behalf),
- the content has never been monetized before (each piece of content can be monetized **once, ever**; this is enforced by a permanent duplicate-prevention registry that survives even account deletion),
- the selected business is participating in the Monetization Program and has available funding (sufficient budget, an active pay-as-you-go card, or platform sponsorship), and
- you own or control all rights needed to monetize the content.

**Business reference requirement.** Your content must genuinely promote the business you select and must **visibly reference it** — for example its name, location tag, Instagram handle, or clearly legible text. An automated check verifies this at creation, and **Share creation is refused when the reference cannot be confirmed.** You additionally remain responsible for the content genuinely promoting the business and for including any paid-promotion disclosures Instagram or the law requires.

Eligibility may also vary by business location, category, account characteristics, and operational or regulatory considerations, and is assessed per Share; it must also be **maintained** until the reward is finalized (Section 8). Meeting the eligibility rules does not guarantee Share creation, insights, or any reward. Beyond the automated and manual checks described in these Monetization Terms, Tagly does not independently verify your representations (including ownership, rights, and legal compliance) and relies on them; no check or review transfers responsibility for your content to Tagly.

## 3. Creating a Share

### 3.1 The Flow

A Share is created only after you select a live Story in the app, select the business it promotes, review the Estimated Reward, expressly accept these Monetization Terms, and confirm creation — and after Tagly's systems successfully record the Share. ### 3.2 No Modification or Deletion

Once created, a Share cannot be edited, reassigned to a different business, cancelled, or deleted by you. If the content stops meeting the requirements (Section 8), the Share becomes unrewarded.

### 3.3 Limits

Tagly may limit how many Shares you can create and how often, apply account-level rules, and prevent Share creation for operational, security, or compliance reasons, without prior notice. Posting frequency also affects reward calculations (Section 6).

## 4. Review and Approval

Every Share is **reviewed by the Tagly team** before it becomes visible to the business and before it can be funded or paid:

- **Approval** makes the Share visible to the business (as an anonymous item) and reserves the business's funds (or schedules a pay-as-you-go charge).
- **Rejection** means the Share receives no reward. Rejections lower your internal quality rating (Section 9.3), and the reason is shown with the Share in the app.
- Payment additionally waits at least 24 hours from Share creation, serving as a review and cooling-off window.

Tagly aims to review promptly but is not required to complete any review within a specific timeframe. Review is performed for platform integrity and creates no duty of care or fiduciary obligation toward Users or Advertisers; review decisions are made at Tagly's reasonable discretion, and Section 19 provides EEA Users with reasons and human-review rights.

## 5. Visibility and Insights

### 5.1 Visibility Requirement

Your content must remain publicly accessible on Instagram for its natural visibility window (for Stories, approximately 24 hours from posting). Content that is deleted, archived, hidden, restricted, or otherwise made inaccessible before its statistics are collected results in an unrewarded Share, regardless of the reason.

### 5.2 Insight Collection

Rewards depend entirely on insights retrieved from Instagram. Tagly collects insights **once per Share**, automatically, approximately 24 hours after the content was posted, and will retry a limited number of times (currently up to five attempts) if collection fails. If insights cannot be collected after the retries, the Share is unrewarded. Only insights retrieved directly from Instagram's official API count: no screenshots, manual submissions, or third-party analytics are accepted, and partial or incomplete insights do not produce partial rewards.

### 5.3 Platform Dependency

Insight availability depends on Instagram. Outages, rate limits, policy changes, or suppressed metrics can make insights unavailable, and Tagly bears no responsibility for such failures.

## 6. Estimated Rewards

Before you create a Share, the app shows an **Estimated Reward**: an informational, **non-binding** preview, not earned income or a promise. Where the business's remaining budget limits what can be paid, the estimate is shown as an "up to" amount.

Estimates are produced by a proprietary, dynamic algorithm that may consider, among other factors: your audience size, composition, and geographic distribution; historical performance; content format; your interests and account characteristics; your posting frequency; your internal quality rating; and business-specific factors including available funding. The algorithm, its inputs, and its weights may change at any time without notice, and no partial, proportional, or minimum payout arises from an estimate. The Final Reward can be higher, lower, or zero.

## 7. Final Rewards

### 7.1 Calculation

The Final Reward is calculated only after the visibility window has elapsed and insights have been successfully collected. The calculation uses a recorded snapshot of the collected insights, your audience data, and the applicable multipliers and parameters, each Share being recorded with the algorithm version used.

### 7.2 Finality

Once calculated, a Final Reward is final: it is not recalculated or adjusted based on later changes to insights, algorithms, or your account. A Final Reward of zero, or no Final Reward because insights were unavailable or eligibility failed, is a defined outcome of the program, not an error or breach.

### 7.3 No Entitlement Before Finalization

No entitlement to compensation exists until a Final Reward is calculated and credited. Pending, estimated, or expected rewards are not earned income, and Tagly has no payment obligation before finalization.

## 8. Unrewarded Shares and Forfeiture

### 8.1 When a Share Earns Nothing

A Share receives no reward if, before its reward is finalized:

- the content is deleted, archived, hidden, restricted, or otherwise becomes inaccessible before insight collection,
- insights cannot be collected from Instagram after the standard retries,
- you disconnect your Instagram account, or your Instagram token expires or becomes invalid,
- you request deletion of your Instagram data,
- you delete your Tagly account,
- the Share is rejected in review (Section 4), or a business report is upheld (Section 9),
- reward settlement permanently fails for technical reasons, or
- enforcement action is taken for fraud or abuse (Section 10), or
- any other eligibility requirement of Section 2 ceases to be met before the reward is finalized (for example, the business stops participating or your account is suspended).

Where a Share becomes unrewarded, any reserved business funds are released, and the app shows the Share's status with a reason where one can be shown. Tagly is not obligated to provide individual notifications beyond this.

### 8.2 Effect of Account or Data Deletion

Deleting your account or your Instagram data permanently forfeits all Shares that have not yet received a Final Reward. **Rewards already credited to your balance are not forfeited: on account deletion your remaining balance is paid out to your Stripe account** (see Section 12 and the Terms of Service).

### 8.3 Finality

Forfeited Shares are permanently ineligible for payment and are not reconsidered, reinstated, or compensated. Technical forfeiture events (missing insights, early removal, lost Instagram access, token expiry, deletion) are not subject to dispute; Section 19 preserves EEA review rights for decisions we make.

## 9. Ratings, Reports, and Manual Review

### 9.1 Business Assessments

The business a Share promotes may, exactly once per Share and at any time, either **rate** the Share (a star rating) or **report** it (for example as not genuinely promotional, misleading, inappropriate, or in bad faith). Assessments cannot be changed once made. For platform-sponsored businesses, the Tagly team may perform the assessment instead.

### 9.2 Reports

A pending report pauses payment of the Share. Every report is manually reviewed by the Tagly team. If the report is **upheld**, the Share is forfeited in full and your quality rating is lowered (repeated upheld reports have escalating effect). If it is **dismissed**, the Share proceeds normally through reward calculation. Review outcomes are final, subject to Section 19; reporting alone never deletes a Share or removes your content from Instagram. Tagly bears no liability for review outcomes or for reports submitted in good faith.

### 9.3 Your Quality Rating

Your account carries an internal quality multiplier that influences future reward calculations within fixed bounds. It is adjusted by business ratings of your Shares, by rejections in review, and by upheld reports. You are notified in the app when an assessment changes it. It affects future rewards only, never a Share already finalized.
